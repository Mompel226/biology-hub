# Where the door images came from

Every image is either in the public domain, released under a Creative Commons licence that
allows this use, or Dr Mompel's own. Each is credited on the page itself, in the colophon.
The files here are resized copies (900, 1400 and 1800 px wide, JPEG and WebP); the sources
below are the originals.

| Door | File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations | `foundations-*` | **HeLa cells** — multiphoton fluorescence, microtubules (magenta) and DNA (cyan). National Center for Microscopy and Imaging Research (NCMIR), via the [NIGMS Image Gallery](https://nigms.nih.gov/image-gallery/3520), image 3520. | [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/) — non-commercial, credited, shared under the same terms |
| The human body | `human-body-*` | **Circulating human blood** — scanning electron micrograph by Bruce Wetzel and Harry Schaefer, National Cancer Institute, 1982. Via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SEM_blood_cells.jpg). The original is greyscale; it is shown here as a red duotone. | Public domain (work of the US Federal Government) |
| Plants | `plants-*` | **Cross-section of a curled marram-grass leaf** (*Ammophila arenaria*) at 100×. Berkshire Community College Bioscience Image Library, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ammophila_arenaria_leaf_cross_section.jpg). | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Life on Earth | `life-on-earth-*` | **The Champagne vent field at NW Eifuku seamount, venting droplets of liquid CO₂.** "Image courtesy of Submarine Ring of Fire 2014 – Ironman, NOAA/PMEL, NSF." [NOAA Ocean Exploration](https://archive.oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/explorations/14fire/background/missionplan/media/eifuku_champagne_vent.html). | Public domain (NOAA) — attribution appreciated and given |
| BioGuardians | `bioguardians-*` | **The BioGuardians banner** — one framed creature to each letter. Made by Dr Daniel Mompel Riera for the BioGuardians CCA at NLCS Jeju. | © Daniel Mompel Riera, used with permission |

To swap an image, drop new files with the same names into this folder (all three widths,
both formats — `python3` with Pillow will make them from one original) and rewrite its row here.
